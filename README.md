# Zone4 — Amazon affiliate product research (verified)

A GitHub Pages site of specification-led buying guides and comparisons for five kitchen products
(three air fryers, two meat thermometers), plus the line-by-line verification record behind every
number it prints. Every value comes from an official manufacturer, Amazon listing or Amazon policy
page. Unconfirmed values are left blank and flagged — never inferred.

**Live site:** https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/Zone4/ · **Summary:** [EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md](EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md)

All links are constructed Associates Special Links carrying the owner-supplied tracking ID
`shopnow047c-20` (destination-tested 2026-11-17); attribution validation against Associates reports is
the owner's gate (F-03). The supplied `link.amazon` short links are kept in the register — their
compliance status is unresolved (FLG-003) and they are not called invalid.

### Engine (`engine/`)

A publish gate + document assembler used by the CI check. Unverified items stay in the review queue and
are blocked from publishing (`ready` defaults to `False`); prices are never rendered unless the owner
exempts an ASIN and the source is official; every claim must carry an `official_source` URL whose host is
in the allowlist (amazon.com, manufacturer sites, ftc.gov, link.amazon, github.com). Its
`tracking` module refuses synthetic sources by test. **Currently 0/7 items are publish-ready — by
design: all seven carry unverified fields and must be resolved or owner-approved first.**

### Site (`docs/`)

Static HTML generated by `scripts/build_site.py` from the data registers: homepage with a three-question
chooser, all-products table with product-intent confirmation record and the derived counter-space
comparison, four category hubs (including meat thermometers), 8 guide/comparison articles, verification
log, flagged-for-review, suggestions, decisions, questions, marketing map, policy compliance (with the
publish gate), rates (blank + provenance), analytics (local-only event counter + five-event schema +
empty campaign ledger), and design system. `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and the RSS `feed.xml` are
regenerated at every build. `tracker.js` records the three site-side events in `localStorage` only —
it makes **no network request** (tested). Shared CSS in `docs/styles.css`.

## Data style

All JSON stores use `json.dumps(..., indent=2, ensure_ascii=False)` with a trailing newline.
Prefer enumerations and explicit units; do not add price or rating fields.

## Limits

- Prices, ratings and review counts change quickly and vary by variant and session; they are withheld
  under F-02 and never scraped at runtime.
- Commission rates are not inferable from public pages and remain blank (F-01).
- Product variants (size/color) exist on several listings; the intended variant of each submitted link
  is flagged (F-57, F-59), not guessed.
- The analytics counter is local-only by design; conversions and commissions come from official
  Associates reports and are never estimated here.
