A-16Organic growth and paid search are governed by different parts of the same policy document, and the paid route is much narrower than most affiliate playbooks assume.Participation Requirement 6(f) permits buying paid search advertisements, but not on Proprietary Terms (Amazon Marks and their variations or misspellings), and the resulting advertisement must send users to your own site rather than directly or indirectly to an Amazon Site. Separately, Commission Income Statement 2(c), 2(d) and 2(e) disqualify purchases referred by a prohibited paid search placement, by a link displayed on a search engine, and by a redirecting link. The practical consequence is that a campaign built on brand-adjacent keywords would disqualify the very purchases it produces, which is why the Suggestions board proposes organic-first.VerifiedS-IPL, S-PEER, S-POL, S-PP, S-TG
