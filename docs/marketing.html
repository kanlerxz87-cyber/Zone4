House rules the drafts follow

  • Only attributes visible in the verified listing title, the brand byline or the category breadcrumb are asserted as facts (R-18).
  • Prices, discounts, star ratings, review counts, badges and sales figures never appear (R-02, R-03, F-02, F-15).
  • No first-person testing, tasting or long-term-use language, and no claim needing proof the advertiser does not have (R-19).
  • The Operating Agreement disclosure sentence sits above the fold on the page and again immediately before each affiliate link (R-01, R-16).
  • Every link is a tagged Special Link to a Product page, never to a search or department page, and no link is placed on Amazon (R-04, R-22, R-23).
  • Promotional copy that mentions a limited-time offer carries an expiry task; these drafts mention none (R-20).
