Executive summary

Three user-supplied links resolve to three verified air-fryer product pages, and every value published on this site is cited to the official page it was read from. Prices, ratings and trend claims stay withheld or flagged, the Kitchen rate is published at category level only, and the next moves — brand, voice, topics, measurement — are laid out as options to choose, not changes already made.
