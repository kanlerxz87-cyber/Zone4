Executive summary

Three user-supplied Amazon links resolve to three air-fryer product pages, re-checked on 2026-11-14. Prices, ratings, commission rates and trend data are not published, because they could not be verified or the Associates policy does not allow them on this site.

What the verification found

  • Identity verified: each link resolves to an Amazon product page (ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH). The title, byline or store, breadcrumb and specification table were matched to the page. All three sit under the same Amazon category path (Home & Kitchen > Kitchen & Dining > Small Appliances > Fryers > Air Fryers). Flag F-04 covers the short-link codes.
  • Pricing and ratings withheld: the Associates Participation Requirements (Section 2(b) and Section 3(t)) allow prices and star ratings on a site only through Amazon-served links or Creators API / PA API. The pages were read, but no price, rating or review count is published (F-02).
  • Commission potential not calculable: the category commission schedule is behind an Amazon sign-in, so no rate is entered (F-01). Third-party sources conflict on the Kitchen rate.
  • Tagging: Participation Requirements §2(b) require the Associates ID (tag) on every link from the site to an Amazon site. The site's source and competitor links carry no tag (F-18), and the short links show no tag (F-03). No Associates ID was supplied, so none was added.
  • Attribution unverified: the resolved links contain no Associates tag parameter (F-03). Confirm the links were generated under your Associates ID before relying on any earnings.
  • Compliance: the required disclosure sentence appears on every page (R-01). Open legal items: the Conditions of Use and the Associates IP License limit collecting listing content, data mining and using Amazon content for AI or ML models (F-11, F-12). The AI content automation is therefore NOT built.
  • Not built: automated product research, PA-API integration, click and conversion tracking, AI article generation and campaign optimisation. Each needs PA-API or Associates reporting access, a rate source, or a compliance decision first.
  • Site: static GitHub Pages site, served from main '/' through a root redirect to docs/ (F-13). Every table row links to an official source, and unverified values are blank and flagged.
