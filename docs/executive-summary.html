Executive summary

Three user-supplied Amazon links resolve to three verified air-fryer product pages. Prices, ratings, commission rates and trend data are not published, because they could not be verified or the Associates policy does not allow them on this site.

  • Re-verification (2026-11-14): each short link was resolved again; titles, breadcrumbs and also-viewed ASINs match the records. The Agreement's disclosure wording, the Participation Requirements text, the Conditions of Use licence and the FTC and Associates guidance were re-read. Corrections: policy section numbers (F-17), the 'Sponsored' label observations (F-10), and competitor labels marked as paraphrased (F-20). Brand bylines and specification tables were not re-read (F-18). The automation conflict with Program Policies is flagged (F-19).

  • Pricing and ratings withheld: the Associates Participation Requirements (Section 2(b) and Section 6(t)) allow prices and star ratings on a site only through Amazon-served links or Creators API / PA API. The pages were read, but no price, rating or review count is published (F-02).
  • Commission potential not calculable: the category commission schedule is behind an Amazon sign-in, so no rate is entered (F-01). Third-party sources conflict on the Kitchen rate.
  • Attribution unverified: the resolved links contain no Associates tag parameter (F-03). Confirm the links are generated under your Associates ID before relying on any earnings.
  • Compliance: the required disclosure sentence appears on every page (R-01). Open legal items: the Conditions of Use limit collecting listing content, data mining and using Amazon content for AI models (F-11, F-12). The AI content automation is therefore NOT built.
  • Not built: automated product research, PA-API integration, click and conversion tracking, AI article generation and campaign optimisation. Each needs PA-API or Associates reporting access, a rate source, or a compliance decision first.
  • Site: static GitHub Pages site (source: main, path /; the root index.html redirects to docs/). Every table row links to an official source, and unverified values are blank and flagged.
