How the gaps get closed

A discovery plan, grouped by who can actually supply the answer. No amount of reading closes a gap that only a person or an account can.

  • From your Associates account — the tag on the links (Q-01), API eligibility (Q-02) and, once signed in, the commission category behind Q-03. Five minutes in Associates Central answers three of the four gating gaps.
  • From Amazon’s official pages, by re-reading — every “Known” row above. The re-read dates and the method are recorded in the sources registry; drift shows up as flags (F-29, F-44, F-47).
  • From a seller or the brand — the P2 function-count discrepancy (Q-15) and product intent (Q-09) need a question asked of a person, not a page read.
  • From counsel — automated collection (Q-07) and liability for listed specs (Q-08). Until then the engine stays offline and research stays manual (D-34).
