Say plainly who writes the site, and how the content is produced

No page states who is responsible for the content, and the articles read as machine output: identical structure, identical sentence rhythm, the same hedged qualifier in every piece. Google's guidance on people-first content asks who created a page, how it was created, and why, and treats unmanaged generative output as low effort; it also warns that fabricated author identities are a deception signal.
