The campaign ledger (empty by design)

The rollup schema the first authorized report is typed into: six columns, the five defined event types, and a dated provenance trail. Rows are added only when their numbers exist in an authorized first-party report — an Associates Central export or this site’s own analytics export — and each row records the report it came from. No conversion score and no revenue forecast is published anywhere: Operating Agreement Section 4 states Amazon makes no representation about the traffic or commission income an Associate can expect (re-read verbatim 2026-11-17). The raw event ledger underneath is data/tracking.jsonl, and engine.tracking refuses synthetic events by design.

Connecting real measurement

  • The owner-selected tracking ID (shopnow047c-20) is already on every outbound product link; confirm its registration in Associates Central with the one-link-per-product test before relying on any earnings (see F-55).
  • Authoritative click, order and commission figures come from the official Associates Central reporting; a rollup row is entered only from such a report, with its source and date (see F-19).
  • Product data served from Amazon must come through the Creators API, and reusing or fine-tuning on API content needs express prior written approval (see F-24).
  • Price tracking and price alerting are prohibited unless Amazon agrees otherwise, so no price history is collected here (see F-22).
  • Add a cookie or consent notice appropriate to your audience before enabling measurement beyond the browser-local ledger (decision D-40 lists the endpoint options; D-16/Q-17 hold the privacy basis), consistent with the disclosure expectations in the official Program Policies and applicable law.
