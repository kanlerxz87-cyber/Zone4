Reader capture: the promise, and why there is no form yet

Reader capture would let a guide's reader be told when the next one is published. That is worth having, and it is a consent contract: a form that posts to no service collects a promise the site cannot keep. So the promise is written here and the form waits for a service with its own privacy policy, a consent record and a working unsubscribe.

  • What would be sent: one email when a new guide is published. Nothing else.
  • How often: at most once a week, on the publishing cadence above.
  • What would never be sent: price alerts, product offers or anything about a specific Amazon listing - Program Policies section 4 governs promoting Amazon products by email and requires an opt-in.
  • How to stop: one click, in every email.
