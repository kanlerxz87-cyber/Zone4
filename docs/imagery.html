Imagery: every picture on this site, and where it comes from

A shop needs pictures. This one shows only pictures it can source: original diagrams drawn from the dimensions the listings state. Nothing here is downloaded from a listing, and nothing is generated to look like the product.

The rule

Listing photography is Program Content. The Operating Agreement gives the store the right to the images on its listings; it does not hand that right to a site that copies them, so none is copied here (suggestion S-18, flag F-43). A generated picture of a real appliance would be worse than no picture, because it would show something the reader cannot buy. What is left is the honest middle: draw the one thing a photograph cannot show as clearly as a diagram — how much counter the appliance takes.

  • Listing images: Not used. Downloaded or hosted listing photography is Program Content and is out of scope for this site (suggestion S-18; flag F-43).
  • Generated images: Not used for products. An AI-generated picture of a real appliance would misrepresent it, so product imagery stays absent until an authorised route exists.
  • Shipped now: Original inline SVG footprint diagrams, drawn to scale from the dimensions already recorded in data/products.json, captioned with the numbers used and labelled as drawn from the listing's product details table. They ship on the four category tiles and at the top of each category page (added 2026-11-19).

What would let a real photograph appear

Either route is an owner decision, not an editorial one: supply original photographs with a written release, or confirm in writing that the Creators API permits live listing images. Both are recorded on the Review page as questions RQ-03 and RQ-05.
