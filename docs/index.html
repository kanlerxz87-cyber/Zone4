Which air fryer is for you? Three questions

Answer three questions and the chooser routes you to the format that fits - using only listed specifications, with the reasoning shown. It is editorial routing, not a market claim: every figure it uses is a sourced row on the product records.

1. How many people are you cooking for?
2. How much counter depth do you have? (measure front to back)
3. Do you want two different foods finishing at the same time?

The routing, in plain text

The same logic the chooser runs, in a table you can check without scripts.

One big dish at a time, and the shallowest footprint you can getThe 8-quart single basket8 quarts; 12.75"D as listed - the shallowest of the three
