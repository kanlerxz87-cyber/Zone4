Chefman 8 Quart Single Basket Air Fryer: what the specification table shows

Verified specifications for the Chefman 8 quart single basket air fryer: capacity, dimensions, presets, temperature range, material and the size variants in the family.

A single basket air fryer cooks in one basket rather than two. The trade is a smaller cooking area than a dual basket unit in exchange for a shorter body, a simpler set of controls and a price point that usually sits below the dual basket models. Inside this set it is the direct alternative to the dual basket oven. M-P3-F2

The listing covered here is the Chefman Air Fryer 8 Qt, filed under ASIN B08DKYBTPH and placed in the Air Fryers category. The listing title describes it as a four-in-one unit that bakes, dehydrates and cooks frozen food, with an XL non-stick dishwasher-safe basket in stainless steel. M-P3-F8M-P3-F9M-P3-F1M-P3-F12

Amazon's product details table lists the capacity as 8 quarts, a footprint of 12.75 inches deep by 13.5 inches wide by 10 inches high, a colour of Stainless steel and a material of Plastic and Stainless Steel. The listed special features are Automatic Shut-Off, LED Shake Reminder and Programmable. M-P3-F3M-P3-F4M-P3-F5M-P3-F6M-P3-F7

One point is worth flagging for buyers: this is a size-variant family, not a single product. The same page offers 6, 8 and 9 quart options, and the supplied link resolves specifically to the 8 quart variant. Every figure on this page therefore describes the 8 quart variant and not the other two sizes. M-P3-F8

For everyday use the listing describes four presets, a touch control with adjustable time and temperature, a temperature range of 200 to 400 degrees Fahrenheit, a dishwasher-safe non-stick basket and flat tray, and a cETL listing with a one-year warranty from Chefman at 1000 watts on 120 volts. The page also shows a newer model of this item in the same family. M-P3-F2M-P3-F9
