Dual basket, single basket or toaster oven: comparing three countertop air fryers

Side-by-side comparison of three countertop air fryers on capacity, footprint, material and format, using only verified listing specifications.

Three countertop air fryers sit in this comparison, and they are not three sizes of the same machine. One is a dual basket oven that cooks two dishes at once. One is a single basket fryer with a four-in-one function list. One is a French door toaster oven that also air fries. Choosing between them is a decision about format and footprint, not about a single specification. M-CMP-F1M-CMP-F2M-CMP-F3M-CMP-F6

On capacity the three listings quote different units. The dual basket oven's product details table says 6 liters while its title says 6 quart. The single basket fryer says 8 quarts in both places. The toaster oven's table says 24.6 liters while its title says 26QT. Because the pages state both conventions, this comparison prints each value with its unit and converts nothing. A reader who wants a like-for-like number should compare basket counts and household size instead. M-CMP-F1M-CMP-F2M-CMP-F3

Footprint separates them cleanly. The dual basket oven measures 13.38 by 11.81 by 10.63 inches. The single basket fryer is 12.75 by 13.5 by 10 inches. The toaster oven is 16.9 by 15.8 by 14.8 inches, which is the largest of the three by a clear margin and is usually the deciding factor when a second appliance has to share the counter. Anyone comparing on price should open the product page, where the current figures are shown by the retailer itself. M-CMP-F4

Amazon's recommendation widgets point the three listings at each other's main rivals: the dual basket oven sits beside another Chefman dual basket model, a Gourmia 10 quart dual basket and a Nutrichef 8.8 quart double basket; the single basket fryer sits beside two compact Chefman 6 quart models and a Cosori 6 quart 9-in-1; the toaster oven sits beside a 25 quart Emeril Lagasse dual zone oven, a 20.3 quart Ninja French door oven and a 28 quart HotKing dual-zone unit.
