Chefman 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer Oven: what the specification table shows

Verified specifications for the Chefman 6 quart dual basket air fryer oven: capacity, dimensions, basket format, controls and colour, plus the alternatives readers compare it with.

A dual basket air fryer is a countertop fryer with two separate cooking baskets instead of one. Two baskets means two dishes can be cooked side by side, and a setting that lets the two finish at the same time removes the need to start one dish in advance. The format matters more than any single number on the box, because it changes how a weeknight meal is actually cooked. M-P1-F2

The product covered here is the Chefman 6 quart dual basket air fryer oven, listed under ASIN B0CDY277D3 and filed by Amazon under Home and Kitchen, Kitchen and Dining, Small Appliances, Fryers, Air Fryers. It is the dual-basket member of the three products in this research set. M-P1-F1M-P1-F9M-P1-F10

Amazon's product details table lists the capacity as 6 liters, while the listing title describes the same product as a 6 quart unit. The two numbers are not converted on this page, because the listing does not state which unit the manufacturer intends. Anyone comparing capacity across brands should treat the figure as listed and check it against the physical product before buying. M-P1-F3M-P1-F4

The remaining structured fields are a footprint of 13.38 inches deep by 11.81 inches wide by 10.63 inches high, a colour listed as Black and Gray, and a material listed as Metal and Plastic. The listed special features are a 2 minute button, automatic shut-off, dual zone features, Hi-Fry and Mute. Each of these is quoted as listed rather than interpreted. M-P1-F4M-P1-F5M-P1-F6M-P1-F7

For everyday use the listing describes a digital dual basket temperature range of 200 to 450 degrees Fahrenheit, removable non-stick coated baskets that are dishwasher safe, and a cETL listing with a one-year assurance from Chefman at 120 volts and 1700 watts. The baskets are listed as two independent 3 quart baskets, which is the practical reason to choose this format over a single basket. M-P1-F2M-P1-F8
