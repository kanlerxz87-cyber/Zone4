Why basket count is the first spec to settle

Two baskets sound like a spec-sheet footnote, but they change how a weeknight actually cooks: with two separate baskets instead of one, a main and a side cook side by side, and the listing title also names a Sync Finish option, so both dishes can be aimed to finish together instead of one waiting while the other cools. The two baskets are independent 3-quart units with a combined 6-quart capacity, stated in the listing title itself. That makes this format worth shortlisting for anyone cooking more than one dish at a time, and worth skipping for anyone who mostly reheats single portions. M-P1-F2
