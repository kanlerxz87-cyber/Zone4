What the listings say about cleaning

The dual-basket title says its baskets are nonstick and dishwasher safe. The single-basket title says its basket is XL, nonstick and dishwasher-safe. The stored record for the oven-style model does not contain a dishwasher statement, so that cell is left blank rather than inferred. Read the care section on the listing before you assume the whole appliance is dishwasher safe. M-G6-F1M-G6-F2M-G6-F3

Footprint and height as listed

The listed dimensions, depth by width by height, are 13.38 by 11.81 by 10.63 inches for the dual basket, 12.75 by 13.5 by 10 inches for the single basket, and 16.9 by 15.8 by 14.8 inches for the oven-style record. The single basket is the lowest of the three, which matters if the space sits under upper cabinets. M-G6-F4M-G6-F5M-G6-F6

The footprint trade-off

Multiply depth by width to get the floor area each unit takes: about 158.0 square inches for the dual basket, 172.1 for the single basket, and 267.0 for the oven-style record. The oven-style record takes about 1.55 times the single basket's floor area on these figures. Whether that space is worth it depends on what you cook, which the listings cannot tell you.
