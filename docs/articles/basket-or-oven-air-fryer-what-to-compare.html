Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Basket or oven-style air fryer? Compare the two formats on the four fields a listing actually states - counter depth, usable capacity, what fits inside, and cleanup - using the verified figures for one dual-basket fryer and one French-door oven.

Disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. This article reports listed specifications; no unit was tested, and the shop links are paid links.

The four fields that decide it

Basket or oven-style is the first fork in the air-fryer aisle, and the listings make it harder than it needs to be by advertising capacity in whatever unit suits the product. Four fields decide it, and all four are printed on the listing: listed counter depth, listed capacity in its own unit, what the format can physically hold, and what the maker says about cleaning. What follows compares one dual-basket fryer and one French-door oven on exactly those four fields and nothing else. M-G4-F1M-G4-F2M-G4-F3M-G4-F4

Counter depth first, because it disqualifies

Measure the counter before you read another word. The dual-basket fryer lists 13.38 inches of depth; the French-door oven lists 16.9. That is three and a half inches of difference between two machines that both call themselves air fryers, and on a shallow counter run it is the difference between a usable appliance and one that lives on a chair. A common countertop is 24 inches deep, so both fit a standard run - but a machine placed under a wall cabinet, beside a kettle, or on a trolley needs the real number, not the standard one. Capacity is listed in two units, so compare within a format

The dual basket lists 6 quarts in its title and 6 liters in its item details - the same machine, two units. The oven lists 24.6 liters. This site does not convert between them, because converting on the reader's behalf would launder a listing inconsistency into a clean number that no source actually states. Read the ratio as an ordering rather than a measurement: the oven's listed interior is several times the fryer's listed capacity, and that ordering is what matters, not the decimal places. What the basket format gives you

Two independent 3-quart baskets, a Sync Finish control named in the title, easy-view windows through which you can watch food without pulling the drawer, and a 200 to 450 degree F range with a Hi-Fry option. In practice that is the format for a main and a side finishing at the same time, and for lifting a basket out to shake it. It is not the format for a tray of anything: the shape is a basket, not a shelf. M-G4-F5

What the oven format gives you

French doors that open without pulling the machine forward, presets counted differently in two places on the listing (24 in one title segment, 25 in a feature heading - flagged rather than resolved, F-26), a dual-speed fan and a rotisserie named in the stored title segment. It is the format that can stand in for a toaster oven as well as a fryer, and it asks for 16.9 inches of depth to do it. M-G4-F6M-G4-F4

Cleanup and handling, from the listed material and basket fields

Both listings name parts as dishwasher-safe: the dual basket's two nonstick baskets are, and the single-basket fryer's basket is. Neither listing states that the whole machine is washable, so the honest reading is that the baskets come out and the housings get wiped. The dual basket's listed materials are metal and plastic; the oven's are stainless steel. Where a listing does not state a cleaning fact, this guide does not supply one. M-G4-F8M-G4-F8M-G4-F8

A one-screen checklist

Measure the counter and write the depth down. Decide whether main-and-side-at-once is a weekly need or a nice-to-have; if it is a need, the basket format answers it directly. Decide whether the machine must also replace a toaster or a small oven; if it must, you are shopping in the oven format and the depth comes with it. Check what the listing actually names as dishwasher-safe rather than assuming the whole appliance is. Then open the two listings and compare the same four fields you started with.
