A French-door format with a wide brief

French doors on a countertop oven are not just theatre: they move the opening away from a single drop-down door, which matters on a crowded counter or in a tight corner. This listing describes a French-door multi-function air fryer toaster oven with a dual-speed fan and rotisserie, an interior light, and stated capacity for 4 to 6 people, including a 12-inch pizza and a 6-pound chicken noted in the listing's feature text. Those are the manufacturer's statements, quoted here as statements. M-P2-F2

One listing, two function counts

The listing is filed under ASIN B09B7SB46R, and it contains a discrepancy worth knowing before you buy: the title says 24 cooking functions, while the feature section says 25 cooking pre-sets, naming Ribs, Defrost, Bake, Toast, Chicken, Pizza, Pastry, Slow Cook, Roast, Reheat and Keep Warm among them. Both counts are reported here exactly as written, because a buyer deserves to know the listing has not fully reconciled its own arithmetic. M-P2-F9M-P2-F10

Capacity: two units on one listing

As with the other products in this set, the units do not match across the page: the product details table lists 24.6 liters, while the title describes a 26QT oven. Neither figure is converted here, because the listing does not say which unit the manufacturer intends. Carry the unit with the number into any comparison, and treat any table that silently reconciles the two with suspicion. M-P2-F3M-P2-F9

The physical facts

The listed dimensions are 16.9 inches deep by 15.8 inches wide by 14.8 inches high, the largest footprint of the three products in this set, so measure the counter before shortlisting it. Colour and material are both listed as Stainless Steel. M-P2-F4M-P2-F5M-P2-F6

Controls and the special-features row

The special-features row lists Automatic Shut-Off, Programmable, Temperature Control and Timer, quoted as listed. Combined with the dual-speed fan and rotisserie named in the format description, the page presents a programmable multi-function oven rather than a single-purpose fryer. That is a format observation, not a quality judgment, and the comparison article on this site treats it exactly that way. M-P2-F2M-P2-F7M-P2-F13

How to use the function list

Function counts sell ovens, but the named functions say more than the number does. Whether the true count is the title's 24 or the feature section's 25, the named list spans slow cooking, baking, toasting, roasting and keep-warm alongside air-frying: a range that suits someone replacing several appliances with one box, and a range a single-basket fryer buyer may not need at all.
