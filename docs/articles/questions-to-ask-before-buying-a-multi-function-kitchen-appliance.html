Which functions will you use?

Start with use, not the number. The oven-style record's About this item section lists eleven modes, including Bake, Toast, Pizza, Pastry, Slow Cook, Roast, Reheat and Keep Warm. Pick three you would use each week, then check they are on the list. M-G7-F1

Does the count match the page?

The same oven-style page gives 24 cooking functions in its title and 25 cooking pre-sets in its About this item section. The site does not choose between them. Ask the seller, or read the product page on the day you buy. M-G7-F2M-G7-F3

What is included?

The oven-style title says six cooking accessories are included. Its product description says 7 premium accessories, including an air fry basket, a rotisserie spit and a bake pan. Check the box contents against the listing you bought from. M-G7-F4M-G7-F5

Brand, store and reviewer

The oven-style record lists its brand as Emeril. The product page links to the Emeril Store. A separate customer-review creator block names Innovation IQ. Brand, store and reviewer are different things, so read each one separately. M-G7-F6M-G7-F7M-G7-F8

Check the offer

On the 2026-11-16 read, the default offer on the dual-basket and single-basket pages was a used item sold by Amazon Resale. Check the condition on the listing before you buy. M-G7-F9

How big is it?

The oven-style record's listed dimensions are 16.9 by 15.8 by 14.8 inches, the largest of the three records this site reads. Measure the spot first, then use the cleanup and counter-space guide to weigh the trade-off. M-G7-F10
