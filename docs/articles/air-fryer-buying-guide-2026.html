Air fryer buying guide: start with the format, then the numbers

A decision-first air fryer buying guide: how basket count, listed capacity, countertop footprint and function count change the choice, with verified specifications.

Start by identifying the format on the product page: P1 has two baskets, P3 has a single basket, and P2 is a French-door air-fryer toaster oven. This guide compares those listed formats and specifications without naming a winner. M-BUY-F1

Keep source units attached to the capacity values. The details tables list P1 at 6 liters, P3 at 8 quarts and P2 at 24.6 liters; the P1 title says 6 quart and the P2 title says 26QT. The pages do not reconcile the P1 and P2 differences, so this guide does not convert them. M-BUY-F2

Check the listed dimensions against available counter space: 13.38 by 11.81 by 10.63 inches for P1; 12.75 by 13.5 by 10 inches for P3; and 16.9 by 15.8 by 14.8 inches for P2. P2 is larger on each listed dimension; this guide does not add a clearance estimate. M-BUY-F3

P2’s page contains a count discrepancy: the title says 24 cooking functions, while the feature section says 25 cooking pre-sets and names Ribs, Defrost, Bake, Toast, Chicken, Pizza, Pastry, Slow Cook, Roast, Reheat and Keep Warm. The source does not explain the difference, so both counts remain reported and unresolved (F-26). M-BUY-F4

Two product details deserve a line each. The single basket fryer is a family of 6, 8 and 9 quart sizes on one page, so check which variant the link selects. The toaster oven’s Item details confirm the Emeril brand and Emeril Lagasse manufacturer; the separate open question is the listing’s 24 cooking functions versus 25 pre-sets (F-26). M-BUY-F5M-BUY-F6

One thing this guide will not do is quote prices, ratings or review counts. Amazon's Associates rules allow a site to publish prices only when Amazon serves the link or when the data comes from an approved Amazon API, and they allow customer ratings and reviews only through that API. Neither route is connected to this site, so those figures are left out and the product page remains the place where the current numbers are shown by the retailer itself. M-BUY-F7
