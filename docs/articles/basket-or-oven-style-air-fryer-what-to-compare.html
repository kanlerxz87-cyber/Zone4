Basket count and format

The dual-basket record's title lists two independent 3-quart nonstick baskets. The single-basket record lists one 8-quart basket. The oven-style record is a French-door multi-function air fryer toaster oven, a different kind of appliance from either basket. Two dishes at once points to the dual basket; one large dish points to the single basket. M-G4-F1M-G4-F2M-G4-F3

Capacity in one unit

Capacity is where one record disagrees with itself. The dual-basket title says 6 Quart and its capacity field says 6 liters. Those are not the same size: 6 liters is about 6.34 U.S. liquid quarts, using the NIST factor of 1 U.S. liquid quart = 0.9463529 liter. The site shows both figures and does not choose one. Footprint as listed

The listed dimensions, depth by width by height, are 13.38 by 11.81 by 10.63 inches for the dual basket, 12.75 by 13.5 by 10 inches for the single basket, and 16.9 by 15.8 by 14.8 inches for the oven-style record. The oven-style record is the largest of the three on every dimension. M-G4-F6M-G4-F7M-G4-F8

Function counts on the page

The oven-style record's title says 24 cooking functions. Its About this item section says 25 cooking pre-sets, and lists eleven modes including Bake, Toast, Pizza, Pastry, Slow Cook and Roast. A 2026-11-15 read of the same page showed the title as '24 One-Touch Preset Options'. The page does not explain the difference, so the site reports both and does not pick one. M-G4-F10M-G4-F11
