A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Check the unit before the number

Start with the unit. The dual-basket title says 6 Quart and its capacity field says 6 liters. Six liters is about 6.34 U.S. liquid quarts, using the NIST factor 1 U.S. liquid quart = 0.9463529 liter. Write every figure in one unit before you compare two machines. M-G5-F1M-G5-F2M-G5-F3

Count the baskets

The dual-basket title lists two independent 3-quart baskets, which add up to the 6-quart figure in the same title. Each basket is smaller than the combined total. A single-basket record listed at 8 quarts gives one space of that size. M-G5-F4M-G5-F5

Check the oven-style figure

The oven-style title says 26QT and its capacity field says 24.6 liters. These agree: 26 U.S. liquid quarts is about 24.6 liters on the NIST factor. So the two figures describe the same capacity in different units. M-G5-F6M-G5-F7

Read capacity next to footprint

Divide each capacity by the listed footprint (depth times width) to compare the room each record gives per square inch of counter. Dual basket: 6 quarts over 158.0 square inches is about 0.038 quarts per square inch. Single basket: 8 quarts over 172.1 square inches is about 0.046. Oven-style: 26 quarts over 267.0 square inches is about 0.097. This is arithmetic on listed figures. It does not measure how well any of them cooks. M-G5-F8M-G5-F9M-G5-F10
