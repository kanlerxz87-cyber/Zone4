How much counter space and cleanup does an air fryer really take?

Every air fryer trades counter space for capacity. The listings give you the footprint and some cleanup facts; the rest is for you to measure.

The footprint, as recorded

P1's recorded dimensions are 13.38 inches deep, 11.81 inches wide and 10.63 inches high [F12]. P3's are 12.75 inches deep, 13.5 inches wide and 10 inches high [F13]. P2's are 16.9 inches deep, 15.8 inches wide and 14.8 inches high [F4].

On these figures P3 is the shallowest of the three and P2 the deepest and tallest. Depth matters for a counter against a wall; height matters for an upper cabinet above the unit.

Cleanup features the titles state

P1's title names nonstick baskets that are dishwasher safe [F1]. P3's title names an XL nonstick basket that is dishwasher safe [F14]. Neither title says anything about the drawer or the heating element, so check the listing for those before you rely on them.

A dishwasher-safe basket is a claim on the listing. We record it as stated and do not test it.

Trade-offs to weigh

1. Count the space you need on both sides of the unit for air flow and for opening it. The recorded dimensions do not include clearance.

2. Decide whether the counter space pays for itself: a larger unit that replaces a toaster oven can free a different space, a basket unit may fit where an oven will not.

3. Check what the listing says about cleaning before you buy, and read the flagged items below for anything we could not confirm.
