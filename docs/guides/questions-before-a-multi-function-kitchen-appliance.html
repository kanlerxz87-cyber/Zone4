What should you ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance?

A multi-function appliance promises to replace several others. Ask these questions first, and check every number against the official listing.

Six questions to ask first

1. Which of the jobs you do now will it replace, and which will you still need a second appliance for?

2. How many functions does the listing claim, and does the spec table give the same number?

3. What is the footprint, and where will it sit when it is switched on and running hot?

4. What is the capacity in the unit you think in, and does the title agree with the spec?

5. What do the cleaning and the parts look like, and does the listing say whether any are dishwasher safe?

6. What does the listing leave out that you would need to know? Write that down before you check out.

An example from one listing

P2's title describes 24 One-Touch Preset Options [F15]. Its 'About this item' section heads its list as 25 cooking pre-sets, so the page gives two counts. We recorded that disagreement as a flag rather than picking a number (F-26). This is exactly the kind of gap question 2 is for.

Our recorded capacity for P2 is 24.6 liters, while its title says 26QT [F9][F3]. The title uses quarts and the record uses liters. Check the listing before you rely on either figure.

What this guide does not claim
