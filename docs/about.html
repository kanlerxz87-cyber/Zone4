About this site

Who writes it, how each number is checked, what it deliberately will not publish, and how to correct it.

What this site is

Zone4 is a small buying guide for countertop air fryers and, since the 2026-11-19 brief, two submitted meat-thermometer listings. It covers a handful of products rather than a catalogue, and it writes about the specification fields a shopper can check against the listing: capacity, footprint, basket format and the features the page names.

It earns through the Amazon Associates programme. Every outbound product link is a tagged Special Link, carries rel="sponsored", and sits next to the disclosure sentence that also appears under the masthead and in every footer.

How the content is produced

Every number on this site was read from an official page and is linked to it in the same paragraph. Where a listing contradicts itself, both readings are published. The site does not pick the tidier one. Nothing is inferred: if the listing does not say what a feature does, neither does the review. Prices, star ratings and review counts are not republished. They are visible on the listing, one click away. Nobody here has cooked in these appliances, and no page claims otherwise. Every gap, defect and unverified value is listed on the Flagged for Review page with its source.

The site publishes with automated assistance and says so here rather than in a footnote. What the automation does not do is invent a number: the build refuses to publish a table row without an official source link, and the checks in the repository fail if one appears.

What it will not publish

  • Prices and star ratings, until an approved API supplies them (flag F-02).
  • Per-product commission rates, because no official source maps these listings to a commission category (flag F-01).
  • Hands-on test results. Nobody here has cooked in these appliances, and the site does not pretend otherwise.
  • Trend or popularity claims. A claim like that needs a dated official snapshot, and none exists (decision D-19, flag F-15).
  • Forecasts of traffic, orders or revenue. The programme makes no representation about any of them.

Corrections

Send a correction with the URL and the listing or policy page that contradicts it. A corrected value is re-read from the official page and the row keeps its new date; nothing is silently overwritten.

Every published value, the page it was read from and the date of the read are listed on the verification log; anything that could not be confirmed is on the Flagged for review page.

How the site is governed

The editorial and technical backlog is public: 73 suggestions, 58 questions, 49 decisions (37 recorded, 12 open) and 88 flags. The two review surfaces are the Review page (the proposals and the questions that gate them) and the Decision centre (the same registers in review order).
