## Direction for the next design prompt

> Design a warm kitchen buying publication. Below the masthead and disclosure, show a clear buying question, one primary comparison action and a restrained editorial image. Use real listing-backed distinctions on product cards; keep illustrations clearly separate from product photography and never imply first-hand testing. On mobile, put the heading and actions before decorative art. Keep the research dashboard one group away and repeat the disclosure in the footer. Prioritize three editorial formats: kitchen-fit checks, capacity comparisons, and cleanup trade-offs. Treat every headline as a hypothesis until query data gives a reason to expand it.
